GOLD LAKEHOUSE FEATURE STORE

10 Microstructural Signal Dimensions

Engineered rolling calculations that translate chaotic order streams into discriminative mathematical fraud vectors.

1. Volume Spike Ratio
Current Vol / 10-Min Rolling Avg Vol. Isolates liquidity pump injections.
2. Cancel-to-Trade Ratio
Cancelled / Total Orders. Catches non-bona fide phantom bids (Spoofing).
3. Orders Per Minute
60-Second Order Velocity. Identifies HFT bot flooding and stacking.
4. Price Deviation %
|Price - FairValue| / FairValue. Isolates benchmark distortion trades.
5. Wash Trade Flag
Circular buy/sell self-dealing indicator between matched entities.
6. Layering Flag
Multi-level order book depth stacking and rapid cancel sequences.
7. Buy/Sell Imbalance
|Buys - Sells| / (Buys + Sells). Uncovers asymmetric order book pressure.
8. 24h Volatility Range %
High - Low / Close. Prevents false alarms during macro market turbulence.
9. Notional Volume
Absolute capital value transacted in the single order packet.
10. Fair Execution Price
Prevailing mid-market benchmark price level at time of arrival.
