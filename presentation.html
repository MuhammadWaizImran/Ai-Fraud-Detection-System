Market Vulnerabilities vs AI Defense
The Problem & Our AI Solution
Why traditional post-trade T+1 auditing fails and how streaming ML solves it.
🚨 THE PROBLEM: Market Manipulation Epidemic
• T+1 Delayed Batch Analysis
Traditional systems run overnight batch reports. By the time fraud is identified, market manipulators have already exited with profits.
• Spoofing & Phantom Orders
Manipulators flood the order book with massive fake orders to skew prices, cancelling them in milliseconds before execution.
• Wash Trading & Self-Dealing
Collusive entities execute simultaneous buy/sell orders between puppet accounts to fabricate fake volume and lure retail liquidity.
• Multi-Layered Attacks Evading Static Rules
Threshold rules (e.g. volume > $1M) miss sophisticated distributed bot attacks spread across hundreds of smaller transactions.
🛡️ OUR SOLUTION: Real-Time AI Intelligence

✔ 3-Model AI Hybrid Ensemble

✔ Dynamic Rolling Feature Engineering

Technology Trade-Off Analysis
Enterprise Tech Stack & Why We Chose It
Comparing each selected tool against industry alternatives and architectural rationale.
⭐ Azure Event Hubs
❌ Alternatives: Kafka Clusters / RabbitMQ
Why Chosen: Fully managed serverless broker, native Azure IAM integration, Kafka 9093 protocol compatibility without cluster maintenance overhead.
⭐ Azure Databricks
❌ Alternatives: AWS EMR / Apache Flink
Why Chosen: Optimized Photon C++ Spark engine (4x faster), Unity Catalog enterprise governance, and serverless compute with scale-to-zero cost efficiency.
⭐ Delta Lake (Medallion)
❌ Alternatives: Raw Parquet / PostgreSQL
Why Chosen: ACID transactions over object storage, Time Travel auditability, streaming checkpoint guarantees, and zero concurrency write conflicts.
⭐ MLflow Model Registry
❌ Alternatives: SageMaker / WandB
Why Chosen: Native Databricks integration, automated Champion-vs-Challenger promotion, strict model versioning, and single-click serving.
⭐ 3-Model AI Ensemble
❌ Alternatives: Static Heuristic Rules
Why Chosen: Static rules fail on novel manipulation. Ensemble marries gradient boosted classification (60%) with unsupervised anomaly detectors (40%).
⭐ Streamlit & Power BI
❌ Alternatives: Tableau / Grafana
Why Chosen: High-frequency real-time WebSocket dashboarding in Streamlit combined with executive enterprise DAX reporting in Power BI.

Medallion Data Lakehouse
Data Evolution: Bronze ➔ Silver ➔ Gold
How raw streaming bytes are refined into certified tabular data and ML feature matrices.
🟫 BRONZE: Raw Stream
Immutable Audit Record
• Format: Unstructured JSON string (json_str)
• Metadata: Kafka Offset, Partition, Enqueued Time
• Data Quality: No filters applied (100% raw)
• Purpose: Legal regulatory audit & data replayability
• Table: bronze.raw_trades
🥈 SILVER: Clean & Validated
Standardized Tabular Relational
• Schema: 11 parsed columns (price, volume, symbol)
• DQ Checks: Positivity (price > 0) & Non-null IDs
• Deduplication: 10-min watermark drops network retries
• Normalization: Lowercase enums & whitespace trim
• Table: silver.clean_trades
🥇 GOLD: Feature Store
Analytics & ML Ready Store
• Features: Volume Spike Ratio, Cancel-to-Trade Ratio
• Temporal Windows: 1-min / 5-min rolling states per trader
• Aggregations: pbi_summary_kpis, pbi_trader_profiles
• Consumption: Feeds AI inference & Power BI dashboards
• Table: gold.pbi_*
